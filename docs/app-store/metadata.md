### Açıklama · Description — 1478/4000 karakter

```
KlariVision is a pitch practice tool for clarinet and other melody instruments in Turkish makam music. It draws the pitch curve of what you play or listen to on top of the degrees of the makam you choose, so you can see which notes are in tune and which drift by a few koma.

LISTENING MODE
• Open an audio or video file (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, MP4, MOV); the pitch curve is ready in seconds.
• Video and graph side by side.
• A/B looping, a countdown before playback, and slow-down playback to 0.10×.
• Follow the curve vertically; zoom time and pitch.
• Analysed recordings stay in your library, where you can edit their name, makam and karar.

PLAYING MODE
• Live pitch graph from your microphone.
• A tuner shows the distance to the nearest degree in koma or cents.
• A signal gate keeps room noise out of the graph.
• Record your practice and save it as a WAV file if you want to.

MAKAMS AND SCALES
• Nihavend, Kürdi, Uşşak, Hicaz, Kürdilihicazkâr, Hicazkâr, Major and Minor.
• Edit makam intervals yourself on the 53-koma octave.
• A Turkish music pitch axis (Sol clarinet notation) and a koma guide.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Note names as Do Re Mi or C D E.
• Three themes and custom graph colours.
• Turkish and English interface.

PRIVACY
All analysis runs on your Mac. No account, no internet connection, no ads and no data collection. Microphone audio is written to disk only when you choose to record.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs; requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
```
